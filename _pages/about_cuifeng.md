[Cuifeng Gao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Tl5aamYAAAAJ&hl=en) is a final-year Ph.D. student at the University of Science and Technology of China, a member of China's C9 League. She received her bachelor's degree from Nanjing Normal University. She is expected to join AIS2Lab as a postdoctoral scholar in Summer 2026 and has been collaborating remotely since August 2025.

She has published in TOSEM 2024, TOSEM 2025, ASE 2024, EMNLP 2025 Findings, and QRS 2025.
She is a key contributor to the Stellar project.

Her research focus is on LLMs for verification and Web3 Fintech security.
