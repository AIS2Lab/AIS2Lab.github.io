[Dr. Cuifeng Gao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Tl5aamYAAAAJ&hl=en) has graduated with a Ph.D. from the University of Science and Technology of China, a member of China's C9 League. She received her bachelor's degree from Nanjing Normal University. She has joined AIS2Lab as a postdoctoral researcher in mid-May 2026 and has been collaborating remotely since August 2025.

She has published in TOSEM 2024, TOSEM 2025, ASE 2024, EMNLP 2025 Findings, and QRS 2025.
She is a key contributor to the Stellar project.

Her research focus is on agentic Web3 Fintech security.
