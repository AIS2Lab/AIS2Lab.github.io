[Dr. Anqi Liang](https://llianga.github.io/) received her Ph.D. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, a member of China's C9 League, and her bachelor's degree from Ocean University of China. She is expected to join AIS2Lab as a postdoctoral scholar in mid-April 2026.

She has published as first author in VLDB 2025, VLDB Journal (VLDBJ) 2024, and Geoinformatica 2024.

Her research focuses on AI-driven trajectory analytics and their applications across various domains, as well as multimodal large language models (MLLMs) and RAG security.
