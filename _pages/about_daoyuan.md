[Prof. Daoyuan Wu](https://daoyuan14.github.io/) is currently an Assistant Professor at the School of Data Science, Lingnan University, where he leads AIS2Lab (Artificial Intelligence and Systems Security Lab). He received his PhD from SMU in 2019 and his MPhil from PolyU in 2015.

Prior to joining Lingnan, Dr. Wu was a Research Assistant Professor (RAP) at HKUST CSE for one year, a Senior Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University for one year, and a Senior Researcher at Huawei HKRC for six months. Before that, he spent three years as a fully independent RAP in the Department of Information Engineering (IE) at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK). He also served as an Adjunct Assistant Professor at CUHK IE for one year, from 2022.09 to 2023.11.

Prof. Wu has published over 30 CCF-A conf papers and around 10 CCF-A journal articles, including one that received the Distinguished Paper Award at NDSS 2025. He has served as a regular TPC member for top-tier security and AI/SE conferences, as well as a Youth Editorial Board Member of the journal Cybersecurity. He has been the PI of the Stellar Academic Research Award, the CCF-Huawei Populus Grove Fund, and several UGC Teaching and Learning Funds. His recent research interests include LLM, Agent, Blockchain, and Code Security. He is a member of ACM, IEEE, and CCF.
